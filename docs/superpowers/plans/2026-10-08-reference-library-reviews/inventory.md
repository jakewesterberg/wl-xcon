## Places where the documents disagree

1. **Panel and viewing distance.** MAP §S0 ("a 32-inch-class 16:9 flat OLED … the display geometry as a formula with
   57 cm as the build target") and CA §16 item 3 ("32-inch-class 16:9 flat OLED … 57 cm build distance") against S0
   §5.1-§5.2, DV §1 and OPT §1: the 27-inch PG27UCDM, the screen fixed 50 cm from the eyes, the stereoscope's path
   about 63 cm (DISP-01, DISP-29, STE-12).
2. **The out-of-cage limit in hours.** M0 §3.1 item 4 ("out of cage to back in cage, twelve hours"), S0 §7.6's storage
   arithmetic (12-hour sessions) and P9 §6 bring-up check 2 ("for 12 hours") against the 8-hour limit in S8 §5.2, SL §6
   and `tasks/eight_hour_bounds.py` (WEL-01). P9 §5 itself notes that S0 §7.6 and its check 2 still use twelve.
3. **Where an aborted trial is repeated, and which.** S8 §2 and §8 item 3, M0 §3.1 item 5, R-RUN §1.5: a fixation break
   is re-queued at the end of the block (fixation breaks only). ENG §16 items 2-3: repeats go to a random later point,
   never the next trial unless only that condition is owed, and fixation break, no fixation, tracker lost and fault
   repeat by default (TSK-29, TSK-30). ENG §22 lists the S8 amendment as pending; R-RUN §5 item 1 records that the code
   matches neither.
4. **One calibration or one per panel half.** S4 §9 and §10 item 3 (a transfer per half of the panel; a gamma ramp per
   half) against ENG §7 item 6 and NOTES §4 Batch 3 (one calibration for the whole panel, eyes assumed equal), which
   says it "changes S4 §9's plan" (COL-11, COL-12).
5. **The stereoscope's alignment check.** S4 §10 item 1, S9 §2 ("optics alignment residual within tolerance") and §6,
   OPT §4.5 and §7, CA §11.3 and ROAD M5: a Nonius or vernier target every session, residual held to a tolerance. ENG
   §12 item 3 and NOTES Batch R5: two monocular 13-point grids, offsets reported, no threshold, the experimenter
   accepts (STE-22, STE-23). ENG §22 lists the S4 §10 amendment as pending.
6. **Color without a measured calibration.** S1a §12 ("Colour without a measured `Calibration` is refused") and PIT
   P19 ("refuse it without a photometer calibration"; "Chromatic tasks will not load until a photometer measurement is
   committed") against ENG §7 items 1-3: a default sRGB calibration on which chromatic tasks other than isoluminant
   ones load, with a warning, in training and piloting; and the instrument is a spectroradiometer, not a photometer
   (COL-01-04, COL-20).
7. **Whose luminosity defines isoluminance.** S1a §12 ("a macaque V(lambda) is not a human one") and PIT P19 ("a human
   V(lambda) makes a stimulus that is isoluminant for nobody in the room") against ENG §7 items 3 and 9: the
   calibration's named observer, by default the human CIE 2006 10° standard, is enough, a per-animal null having been
   declined (COL-03, COL-04, COL-15). R-A2 Q5 still holds the luminosity function open (COL-17).
8. **The luminance bound.** The four reference tasks' luminance `Param`s ("Bounded at 100 until V9 …", `high=100.0`)
   and NOTES "Build A's plans" (100 cd/m²) against NOTES "The morning after" (2026-10-08: 0-80 cd/m² until a panel is
   measured, "lowering the 2026-10-07 bound of 100") (COL-29). Recorded in the commit read; the tasks are not yet changed.
9. **Tracker graces from the paper's numbers.** S1a §13 (tracker-loss grace defaults to 0.05 s, "P6's measured stall
   maximum") and DEMO §3.3 ("the 50 ms staleness rule (`eye.Tracker.staleness`)") against S5 §4 ("Nothing downstream
   may quote the paper's 2% as though it described our rig") and §10 item 2 (ceiling and graces "frozen only after
   V3(a)"), ROAD M3 ("Gaze-window grace parameters set from data, never from the paper's numbers") and CA §9.2
   (EYE-17, EYE-18).
10. **Leaving the target during its hold.** S1a §7 (a hold not maintained is a break: `TARGET_BREAK`) and
    `tasks/visual_search.py` (`Exited("search.target")` → `TARGET_BREAK`) against `tasks/fixation_detection.py` and
    `tasks/adaptive_detection.py` (`Exited("target")` in `verify` → `WRONG_TARGET`), so in the detection tasks a target
    break is scored as a wrong choice: counted, not repeated, and a failure for the staircase (TSK-11, TSK-34, TSK-39).
11. **What moves a staircase.** `tasks/adaptive_detection.py` `next_params` (only correct and wrong-target trials;
    no-response ignored) against ENG §15 item 3 and NOTES §12 Batch 1 (no response is a failure by default)
    (TSK-33, TSK-34). ENG §25 defers the task's own rule to the PI.
12. **A contrast with no convention.** ENG §4.12 (every contrast written with its convention; a bare contrast refused
    once build A migrates the tasks) against `tasks/adaptive_detection.py`'s `contrast` `Param` (unit "fraction", no
    convention; read by no stimulus per R-RUN §5 and DEMO §14 item 2) (COL-24, TSK-34).
13. **What plays sound.** ARCH "The task PC's interface" ("Not in copper: touchscreen and audio output. Both are
    host-side") and CA §3.2 ("Not present in copper: touchscreen, audio output … USB/host-side") against ENG §9 item 1:
    two analog outputs of the PCIe-6343, started by the task-patch edge. Within NOTES, §6 Batch 1 ("the recording card …
    on the recording's own clock") is corrected by Batch R5 (REC-13). ENG §22 lists the S4 and S6 amendments as pending.
14. **The calibration constellation.** S9a §2.2 ("The calibration grid (S5 §7, a 3×3 — never a ring)") against S5 §2
    as amended 2026-09-05 and ENG §12 item 2: thirteen targets (EYE-06).
15. **Where the photodiode patches go.** S3 §10 item 3 ("candidate found (central strip from the nasal clip)") against
    S3 §8, S4 §7, OPT §5 and DV §4: the bottom strip, and in direct view a bottom corner under the housings; OPT §8 item
    4 keeps the central strip only as a fallback (DISP-26).
16. **Which protocol photometers the panel halves.** ARCH "The display" ("Panel left/right nonuniformity … is
    photometered in V1") against S4 §11, S0 §5.4, CA §14 and DV §7 (V9; DV §7 corrected the same attribution for ABL)
    (STE-02, DISP-02).
17. **A trial that reaches the five-minute cap.** ENG §6 item 8 (recorded as `"hang"`) against NOTES §3 Batch 4 ("a
    trial still running at the cap ends as a fault"); a fault is repeated by default (ENG §16 item 2) (DISP-13, TSK-29).
18. **Variation the task docstrings describe and the tasks do not perform.** `tasks/fixation_detection.py`'s docstring
    and S1 §5 ("a target appears at one of six positions") against its single horizontal `target_position`;
    `tasks/visual_search.py`'s `array_phase` "Randomised between trials so the animal cannot learn positions" and
    `tasks/adaptive_detection.py`'s "mini-blocks of held eccentricity", which nothing performs (R-RUN §5 item 10)
    (TSK-34, TSK-37, TSK-39).
19. **Whether the 30-minute warning is settled.** S9 §3 ("The threshold is configurable and its default is a proposal,
    not a settled figure") against S8 §5.2 item 4 ("Accepted by the PI on 2026-09-20 as a starting value, so it is his
    figure") (WEL-05).
